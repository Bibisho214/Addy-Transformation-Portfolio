1. Purpose & Guidance
What is the purpose?
•	A one-page summary to prepare for and drive focused conversation at the QBR (Quarterly Business Review) forum, covering:
•	Progress against Objectives & Key Results, and key commitments/outcomes from the last quarter
•	The most critical work planned looking forward, and how it impacts Objectives & Key Results
•	Key trade-offs and risks that require senior leadership awareness
Who is this for?
•	Teams, programs, or portfolios presenting at the QBR forum
What are the expected outcomes?
•	A succinct, focused discussion at the QBR forum on how committed work contributes to Objectives & Key Results — looking at the past, current, and upcoming quarter
Formatting Guidance
•	Maximum one page / one slide
•	Keep language concise — bullet points, not paragraphs
•	Remove instructional text (like this section) before submission
•	Do not add additional sections or columns beyond this template
