# Enterprise Delivery Governance Model

## Executive Summary

Successful transformation initiatives require more than project execution. Organizations need a governance model that provides visibility, accountability, decision-making authority, and delivery oversight across the entire initiative lifecycle.

This framework defines a structured governance approach for managing strategic initiatives from intake through delivery and business adoption.
---
## Purpose
The Enterprise Delivery Governance Model establishes:
- Delivery oversight
- Executive accountability
- Risk management
- Decision governance
- Delivery assurance
- Strategic alignment
---
## Business Challenges
Common delivery challenges include:
- Unclear project ownership
- Inconsistent delivery practices
- Limited executive visibility
- Poor risk escalation
- Funding uncertainty
- Misalignment between strategy and execution
---
## Governance Principles
### Strategic Alignment
All initiatives must align to strategic business objectives.

### Transparency

Delivery status, risks, and dependencies should be visible to stakeholders.

### Accountability
Decision authority and ownership must be clearly defined.
### Risk-Based Oversight
Governance effort should match initiative complexity and risk profile.
