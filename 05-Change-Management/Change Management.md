What	Change Management Scope of Work Template

Why	Sets expectations early with your People Leader and initiative team on the scope of CM support and deliverables. Use this template when you are first assigned to an initiative, and during initial conversations, to: understand expectations about the assignment; gather information about the scope of CM support/deliverables; and share that understanding with your business lead/initiative team.

5. Considerations Typically Outside CM Scope
Items that typically do not fall within the scope for CM (at the discretion of the People Manager and Practitioner). This is not an exhaustive list — add any deliverables, activities, or engagements you identify or recommend.
•	Representing the business to affected stakeholders — practitioners should facilitate stakeholder engagement activities but not serve as the voice of the business
•	Communications: business as usual communications outside the scope of the initiative, executive communications, creating or overseeing e-mail distribution lists, creating/owning shared or personal e-mail inboxes
•	Owning or managing SharePoint/intranet sites
•	Owning or managing calendar coordination and logistics around events (virtual/in-person) or team huddles/meetings
•	Training: building or delivering training materials
•	Creating or delivering the project plan for the initiative
•	Documenting initiative business requirements
•	Execution of activities in the sustainment plan (e.g., post-launch activities scheduled after the warranty period for CM support), unless CM partners with the initiative team to identify ownership of sustainment activities
